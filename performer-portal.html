Privacy Policy

Information used. SD Karaoke processes account usernames and credentials, room membership and activity, song searches and reservations, and account or room requests to provide sign-in, room access, queue, and moderation features.

Storage. Some session details, device identifiers, saved favorites, and search cache are stored in your browser's local or session storage. Account, room, membership, and request records may be stored in Firebase Realtime Database, depending on the system configuration. Signing out or clearing browser storage does not necessarily delete records stored in Firebase.

Third-party services. Song searches use the YouTube Data API and playback uses the YouTube embedded player. Search and playback requests are handled by Google/YouTube and are subject to their privacy policies.

Developer: Shan Denver. For privacy or account questions, contact your system administrator.

About

SD Karaoke is a browser-based karaoke system for shared rooms. Performers can search YouTube songs and add them to a room's reserved queue, while administrators manage accounts, rooms, and display settings.

Developer: Shan Denver.
